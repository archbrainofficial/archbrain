General Questions

What is ARCHBRAIN?
ARCHBRAIN is Nigeria's first IMEI-based mobile device protection and tracking service. It uses your device's unique hardware identifier (IMEI) to track, protect, and recover stolen phones even after factory reset. Built by Nigerians for Africans, ARCHBRAIN works with African infrastructure and connectivity.
How is ARCHBRAIN different from Find My Phone?
Unlike built-in tracking that gets disabled when thieves factory reset your phone, ARCHBRAIN uses IMEI-based identification which is burned into your phone's hardware and cannot be changed. Our service works across all phone brands and includes remote lock/wipe, official proof of ownership, and recovery assistance.
Is ARCHBRAIN legal?
Absolutely. ARCHBRAIN operates under Nigeria's Cybersecurity Act and complies with international data protection standards. We work closely with law enforcement and telecom regulators. IMEI tracking is a standard industry practice used by governments, carriers, and major tech companies worldwide.
Is my data safe with ARCHBRAIN?
Yes. All data is encrypted end-to-end using AES-256 military-grade encryption. Your IMEI is one-way hashed and cannot be reversed. We comply with GDPR, CCPA, and Nigeria's NDPR. We never sell your data to third parties. All servers are hosted in Africa for data residency compliance.

Registration & Setup

How do I find my IMEI number?
The easiest way: Dial *#06# on your phone and a 15-digit number will appear. Alternatively, check Settings → About Phone → Status → IMEI. For iPhones: Settings → General → About → IMEI. You can also check your phone's box or under the battery.
Can I register multiple devices?
Yes! Free plan: 1 device. Pro plan: 5 devices. Premium plan: Unlimited devices. You can manage all devices from one account and switch between them instantly. Perfect for families or business users.
Do I need GPS turned on?
For accurate real-time tracking, GPS should be enabled. However, ARCHBRAIN can still track your device using cell tower triangulation and WiFi even without GPS. Accuracy will be reduced but you'll still get location within 100-200 meters.
Will ARCHBRAIN drain my battery?
No. We've optimized the app to use minimal battery. It runs in the background with smart power management. On average, ARCHBRAIN uses less than 2% battery per day. You can also disable tracking during certain hours.
What happens if I change my SIM card?
No problem! ARCHBRAIN doesn't depend on your SIM card. It tracks your device by its IMEI hardware ID. Changing your SIM card won't affect tracking. Premium members get alerts when a new SIM is detected.

Tracking & Features

How accurate is the tracking?
GPS accuracy is typically 5-12 meters in open areas. In urban areas with tall buildings, it may be 20-50 meters. Indoors without strong GPS signal, accuracy can be 100+ meters using WiFi and cell towers. More satellites = better accuracy.
How often is my location updated?
Real-time tracking: Every 30 seconds. Standard tracking: Every 5 minutes. You can customize update frequency in settings. More frequent updates use more battery. Pro tip: Increase frequency when looking for a stolen device.
Can I track my device if it's offline?
Not real-time. However, once your device goes online again, you'll immediately receive its last known location and all tracking data. Commands you issue (lock, wipe, etc.) are queued and execute once the device reconnects.
What does "Remote Lock" do?
Remote Lock secures your device with a unique PIN that only you know. Thieves can't access your data. The lock persists even after they try to restart the device. You can unlock it remotely or set it to unlock only with biometric authentication.
What's the difference between Lock and Wipe?
Lock: Secures your device with a PIN. You can unlock it later and recover the device. Wipe: Permanently deletes all data. Use Wipe only if you're certain you can't recover the device. Wipe cannot be reversed.

Device Protection

My device was stolen. What should I do?
1. Open ARCHBRAIN immediately and note your device's location. 2. Lock your device remotely. 3. Call your bank to freeze cards. 4. Report to police with your IMEI proof. 5. Contact your carrier to block the SIM. 6. Premium members get recovery assistance from our team.
Will ARCHBRAIN work after factory reset?
Yes! That's the main advantage of IMEI tracking. Factory reset wipes your apps but not your IMEI hardware identifier. ARCHBRAIN will continue to work and track your device. Once online, the app reinstalls automatically.
Does ARCHBRAIN work internationally?
Yes! Track your device anywhere in the world. Roaming charges may apply when traveling. Premium members get international support. Note: Some countries may have restrictions on tracking. Always comply with local laws.
Can I use ARCHBRAIN with a second-hand phone?
Yes! You can register a second-hand phone to your account. The previous owner's registration will need to be cleared from ARCHBRAIN's servers first (contact support). This prevents theft disputes and ensures only one person can control each device.
