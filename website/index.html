FAQ

Common Questions

Everything users ask before subscribing — answered honestly.

Why am I paying ₦2,500 per month? What exactly am I getting?
Running high-frequency real-time GPS tracking servers requires active cloud database infrastructure. ₦2,500/month gives you instant location updates every 30 seconds, remote lock & wipe capability, 90-day movement history, SMS alert gateways, and priority recovery support in Nigeria.
Can ARCHBRAIN really track my phone after a factory reset?
Yes! Using our Knox & Android Enterprise MDM profile integration, ARCHBRAIN binds directly to system-level permissions. When enrolled, factory resets can be completely blocked or the tracking agent automatically reinstalls upon reboot.
Is ARCHBRAIN spying on me?
Absolutely not. ARCHBRAIN is a lawful anti-theft and device security platform. An active status icon is visible on your phone screen when running, and your data is encrypted. We do not access calls, messages, camera feeds, or personal files.
What happens if I cancel my subscription?
You can cancel anytime with a single tap. Your account gracefully reverts to the Free Starter plan (up to 2 devices, 5-minute tracking interval). No lock-in period or hidden cancellation fees.
What if ARCHBRAIN cannot recover my phone?
While our system boasts a 95% recovery rate across major Nigerian urban centers, if physical recovery is delayed, you can trigger our Remote Wipe feature to permanently destroy all personal data, photos, and banking credentials so thieves get nothing.
Does ARCHBRAIN drain my phone battery?
No. Our mobile application uses intelligent background location services (geofencing and motion sensors) that consume less than 2% of total battery life over a 24-hour period.
How do I pay? Can I pay with my Nigerian bank?
Yes! We accept all major Nigerian debit cards (Mastercard, Visa, Verve), direct bank transfers, and USSD payments through our secure Paystack integration.
Can I track someone else's phone?
No. ARCHBRAIN requires explicit device owner consent during initial app enrollment. Secretly tracking another adult's device without permission is strictly illegal under Nigerian law and NDPR privacy guidelines.
What is the difference between the free and Premium tracking speed?
The Free Starter plan updates locations once every 5 minutes. The Premium plan updates locations every 30 seconds. In 5 minutes, a moving vehicle can travel several kilometers; 30-second tracking provides real-time, street-level accuracy.
Is my data stored in Nigeria?
Yes. ARCHBRAIN servers and data backups strictly comply with NDPR (Nigeria Data Protection Regulation) guidelines and NITDA standards, protected by end-to-end encryption.
