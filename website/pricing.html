Pricing FAQs

Can I upgrade or downgrade anytime?

Yes! Change your plan anytime. Upgrades are prorated. Downgrades take effect at next billing cycle.

Is there a contract or long-term commitment?

No! Cancel anytime with no penalties. Monthly plans give you flexibility. Annual plans have a 7-day money-back guarantee.

What happens if I cancel?

Your device remains registered but you lose access to premium features. Reactivate anytime to continue protection.

Do you offer refunds?

Yes! Annual subscriptions include a 30-day money-back guarantee. Monthly plans are non-refundable after 7 days of use.

Can I use multiple payment methods?

Yes! You can switch between payment methods on your account settings anytime.

Are there family or corporate discounts?

Yes! Contact our sales team for family bundles (2+ subscriptions) and corporate packages.

Do you offer educational discounts?

Yes! Students and teachers get 30% off Pro and Premium plans. Verify your .edu email to claim.

What about taxes or VAT?

Prices shown do not include VAT. VAT will be calculated at checkout based on your location.
