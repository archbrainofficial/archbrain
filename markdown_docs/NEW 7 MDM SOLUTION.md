What is MDM (Mobile Device Management)?

The Simple Analogy

Think of MDM like a remote control for phones.

Just like your TV remote controls your television from a distance, MDM lets you control, monitor, and manage phones remotely — without touching them physically.



Real Life Example

Imagine you own a company called Konga Deliveries with 50 delivery riders. Each rider carries a company phone. Now:

Problems you face without MDM:

❌ A rider steals the phone and runs away

❌ A rider installs games and wastes data

❌ A rider loses the phone with customer data on it

❌ You don't know where your riders are at any time

❌ A rider shares sensitive customer info



Problems MDM solves:

✅ You lock or wipe the stolen phone remotely

✅ You block app installations remotely

✅ You wipe sensitive data remotely

✅ You see all 50 riders on a live map

✅ You control what apps they can use



The 5 Things MDM Does

1. 📡 Track Every Device

You sit in your office in Lagos.

You open ARCHBRAIN dashboard.

You see a map with 50 pins — one for each

company phone — moving in real time.



You can see:

→ Which rider is at which location

→ Which phone hasn't moved in 2 hours

→ Which phone left the approved delivery zone

2. 🔒 Lock Any Device Remotely

A rider reports their phone stolen at 3pm.

You open your dashboard.

You click the phone → click "Lock".

Within 5 seconds, that phone's screen locks.



The thief cannot access:

→ Customer data

→ Company bank apps

→ Delivery platform login

→ Personal contacts

3. 🗑️ Wipe Any Device Remotely

Worst case scenario — the phone cannot

be recovered.



You click "Wipe".

Within seconds, ALL data on that phone

is permanently deleted.



→ No customer data leaked

→ No company secrets exposed

→ Phone is now a blank device

4. 📋 Enforce Rules (Policies)

You create rules that apply to ALL

company phones automatically:



Rule 1: Screen must lock after 2 minutes

Rule 2: Cannot install WhatsApp or games

Rule 3: Must have a 6-digit PIN

Rule 4: Cannot turn off location sharing

Rule 5: Cannot take screenshots of customer data



Every phone obeys these rules — even if

the employee tries to change them.

5. 📊 Monitor Everything

Your dashboard shows you:

→ Battery level of each device

→ Last time each device was active

→ Which apps are installed

→ Data usage per device

→ History of every location visited

Is It Legal?

Yes — with employee consent. This is critical:

LEGAL ✅:

→ Employee knows their work phone is monitored

→ Employee signed consent during setup

→ Monitoring is for work purposes only

→ Personal phones are NEVER monitored



ILLEGAL ❌:

→ Installing MDM on someone's phone secretly

→ Monitoring personal phones without consent

→ Using MDM to track employees after work hours

   on their personal device

ARCHBRAIN enforces this by requiring every employee to go through a consent screen before enrollment.
