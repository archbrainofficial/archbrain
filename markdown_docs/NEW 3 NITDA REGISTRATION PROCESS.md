NITDA REGISTRATION PROCESS

What is NITDA?

The National Information Technology Development Agency regulates IT businesses in Nigeria and enforces the NDPR (Nigeria Data Protection Regulation).

Steps to Register ARCHBRAIN with NITDA

Step 1: Business Registration (CAC)

1. Go to: https://pre.cac.gov.ng

2. Register ARCHBRAIN as a Limited Liability Company

3. Documents needed:

   - Memorandum of Association

   - Articles of Association

   - Directors' details (BVN, ID)

   - Company address

Cost: ₦10,000 - ₦50,000 depending on share capital

Step 2: NITDA Registration

1. Visit: https://nitda.gov.ng

2. Click "Register IT Company"

3. Documents needed:

   - CAC Certificate

   - Tax Identification Number (TIN)

   - Company profile

   - Directors' credentials

   - IT service description

Cost: Varies by company size

Step 3: NDPR Compliance (Critical for ARCHBRAIN)

Since ARCHBRAIN collects personal data (IMEI, location),

you MUST:



1. Appoint a Data Protection Officer (DPO)



2. Conduct Data Protection Impact Assessment (DPIA)



3. File Annual Data Audit Report with NITDA



4. Register as Data Controller at:

   https://ndpr.nitda.gov.ng



5. Implement these in your app:

   ✅ Privacy Policy page

   ✅ Cookie consent

   ✅ Data deletion request feature

   ✅ Data breach notification system

Step 4: NCC (Nigerian Communications Commission)

For IMEI-related services contact NCC:

Website: https://www.ncc.gov.ng

Address: Plot 423, Aguiyi Ironsi Street,

         Maitama, Abuja

Phone: 622 (toll free)



Apply for:

- Value Added Service (VAS) License

- Telecom Service Provider registration

Step 5: FIRS Tax Registration

1. Visit: https://www.firs.gov.ng

2. Register for:

   - Company Income Tax

   - VAT Registration

   - TIN Number
